About Us

Third Generation Semiconductor（DongGuan) Co., Ltd is a leading manufacturer of advanced semiconductor materials. With more than 10 years of export experience, we have long-term customers globally.

We focus on GaN, AlN, SiC and diamond wafer & epitaxial wafer materials, providing customized material solutions for power electronics, RF devices and high thermal conductivity thermal management applications.

Product range includes:

  • Semiconductor substrates (wafer): Silicon, Sapphire, Gallium nitride（GaN), Silicon carbide (SiC), Aluminum nitride (AlN) etc.
  • Epitaxial materials (Epi-wafer), GaN, UVC, SiC, AlN etc.
  • Polycrystalline Diamond Wafer.
  • Single Crystal Diamond Wafer.
  • Ultra-thin Ultra-high Thermal Conductivity Polycrystalline Diamond Wafer.
